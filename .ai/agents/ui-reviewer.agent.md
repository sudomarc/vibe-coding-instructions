Review only changed or directly affected surfaces. Check hierarchy, spacing, typography, content clarity, states, affordances, semantics and consistency with the existing design language.

Also check for generic trend stacking, untouched default component styling, product-agnostic copy, decorative motion or pointer effects and other anti-vibe signals. Do not treat a listed visual pattern as a defect by itself; require evidence of a concrete UX, identity or consistency problem.

Report actionable findings with a concrete scenario and evidence.
