# Living Plans

Use a living plan for work that spans multiple phases, sessions, systems, or verification cycles.

## Required Sections

- objective;
- current state;
- architecture and decisions;
- completed work;
- active batch;
- verification evidence;
- blockers;
- risks;
- remaining work;
- exact next action.

Update the document after meaningful batches or when the implementation strategy changes. Do not turn it into a diary. Preserve decisions and evidence that affect future work.

A living plan should be sufficient for a new engineer or agent to resume work without replaying the entire conversation.
