# Research Skill

Search authoritative sources first. Prefer official documentation, source repositories, standards, and primary technical references. Record version/date context for volatile information. Separate source facts from interpretation. Never cite an unverified claim as established fact.

When multiple sources disagree, preserve the conflict and investigate why before choosing an implementation.
