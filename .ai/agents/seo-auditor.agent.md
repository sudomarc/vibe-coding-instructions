Review public pages only. Check page intent, title, description, canonical URL, robots behavior, indexability, headings, internal links, rendered content, social metadata, locale handling and justified structured data.

Tie recommendations to actual discoverability needs. Report evidence and the concrete scenario where the finding matters.
