Measure or inspect the affected path before optimizing. Check bundles, rendering work, images, fonts, requests, caching, long tasks, rerenders and third-party scripts.

For advanced motion or 3D, inspect render-loop lifetime, frame pacing, device-pixel ratio, texture memory, scene complexity, post-processing, asset decode cost and cleanup.

Separate measured regressions from hypotheses and do not recommend micro-optimizations without a bottleneck.
