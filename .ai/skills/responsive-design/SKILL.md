# Responsive Design Skill

Design around content and constraints rather than device labels.

Inspect containers, breakpoints, grid or flex conventions, typography scaling and overflow rules.

Verify narrow mobile, tablet-like widths, desktop, wide desktop when relevant, intermediate transition widths, long text and translated text.

Prefer fluid sizing and intrinsic layout where they simplify behavior. Do not multiply breakpoints to patch brittle layouts.
